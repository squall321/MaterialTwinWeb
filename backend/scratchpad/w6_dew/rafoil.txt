                                 of Copper Foil Using O2 / Ar Plasma

                        (Received September 6, 2013; Revised October 4, 2013; Accepted October 14, 2013)


    Abstract: In this study, the surface modification of copper foil using an inductively coupled O2 / Ar
    plasma as O2 gas fraction (0∼100%) was investigated in order to improve the surface characteristics.
    After plasma treatment, the measurement of the surface roughness, surface contact angle and surface
    energy were performed for the surface analysis of copper foil. As a result, the surface roughness and the
    surface energy were increased. And plasma diagnostics was performed by a double Langmuir probe
    (DLP) and optical emission spectroscopy (OES). Using these results, the plasma surface modification
    mechanism was investigated.


    Keywords: Copper foil, Roughness, Surface energy, Plasma, O2, Ar, DLP, OES

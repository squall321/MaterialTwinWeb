 Super Insulation
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 This is a vacuum multilayer heat insulating material used as a radiation heat suppression film in vacuum areas for cryogenic and space applications. It demonstrates good thermal insulation performance as a cryogenic MLI* for equipment used near absolute zero, such as superconducting equipment, superconducting power transmission cables, and cryogenic containers, and as a thermal shield to protects satellites and spacecraft from radiant heat. MLI has been used in many space applications, including the “KOUNOTORI” Space Station supply vehicle and the “KIBO” International Space Station. 
 *MLI: Multi-Layer Insulation 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Features 
 
 
 Integrated production from raw material polyimide film APICAL™ in KANEKA.
 
 
 Good heat insulating properties as a vacuum insulation material for cryogenic temperature cryostats
 
 
 Protects the spacecraft and satellites from radiation heat
 
 
 Proven track record in space applications such as H-II Transfer Vehicle “Kounotori”(JAXA) and International Space Station "Kibo" missions
 
 
 Applications 
 
 Multi Layer insulation for superconducting equipment 
 Multi Layer insulation for cryogenic containers 
 Multi Layer insulation for space use

 Thermal Strap for Space Applications 
 
 
 
 
 This is a heat transport material, using multi-layered graphite sheets, to cool heat-generating parts of satellites. 
 Compared to copper and aluminum, this material has a high heat transport capacity and it is lightweight, flexible and bendable.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Features 
 
 
 Multilayer graphite sheet allows for high heat transport (GS) (3 times higher thermal conductivity than copper)
 
 
 Light weight (density 1.8 g/cm³) 　→　1/5 of copper, 2/3 of aluminum
 
 
 Flexible and bendable
 
 
 Low outgas, suitable for outer space use
 
 
 Applications 
 
 Space applications, mainly cooling of heat-generating components of satellites

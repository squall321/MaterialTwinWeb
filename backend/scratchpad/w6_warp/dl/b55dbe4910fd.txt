TECHNICAL DATA SHEET
Avery Dennison™ XD 3827 is part of a range of high technology self-adhesive products,
developed for specific industrial applications.


CONSTRUCTION & TYPICAL APPLICATIONS
● Consists of a special laminate coated with a high performance, pressure sensitive
  acrylic adhesive on one side and protected with a 100 gr PE coated paper liner.
● Utilizes a special laminate that minimizes the transmission of water vapor. It also
  functions as an excellent barrier to a variety of gases and has an excellent anchorage
  on various sealants and adhesives. The adhesive provides very good adhesion to a
  wide variety of substrates and exhibits the good durability and long term aging
  properties.




                                           CONSTRUCTION
                                           Carrier: Barrier Film
                                           Adhesive: Engineered acrylic adhesive
                                           Liner: White PE protected paper




RESISTANCE
● Resistant to water, detergents, alcohol, aliphatic and some aromatic hydrocarbons.
  Medium resistance to plasticizers and high outdoor resistance. Not recommended for
  use in contact with ketones, esters or chlorinated hydrocarbons.

SHELF LIFE
●   2 years when stored at 15/25 ˚C and ± 50 % relative humidity.

Product advantages
 Appropriate initial adhesion (tack), repositionable at short term
 High final strength
 Maximum ageing and UV-resistance
 Resistance against weathering
 The adhesive has good to excellent resistance to oil, grease, cooling lubricants, acids, alkaline solutions, disinfectant, fuel and
  many other chemicals. (According to FINAT FTM 17)
 Excellent flatness and dimensional stability
 Good die-cutting properties
 Easily removable protective liner
 Non-slip liner
 Easy identification because of product marking
 High electrical resistance and dielectrically strength


Main application fields
 Spacer tape for the manufacture of membrane switches
 Bonding of overlay films
 Fixing of membrane switches on supporting plates
 Mounting of face plates, scales, nameplates and signs
 For electrical insulation between electrical / electronic components
 Spacer between battery cells for battery modules

 Grand Opening May 13, 2025 
 In order to collaborate with the decentralized supply plan of European and American OEM and PCB customers, TUC’s board of directors decided to establish a Thai subsidiary on November 1, 2022. TUC plans to further establish a Southeast Asian production base in Chonburi Province, Thailand’s Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC). A new 100-rai (160,000-square-meter) plant. Production is expected to be completed in the first quarter of 2025. The target CCL production capacity of the first stage is 300,000 sheets/month. In the future, dynamic adjustments will be made depending on customer orders and the overall economic situation. The maximum monthly production capacity can reach 900,000-1.2 million sheets.

 Poly Max™ Polyester Acoustic Wall & Ceiling Panels 
 High-Performance Acoustics with Modern Design & Material Integrity 
Poly Max™ acoustic panels provide an elegant and sustainable solution for controlling echo and reverberation across a wide range of interior environments. Engineered for both performance and aesthetics, these panels are ideal for wall and ceiling applications in commercial, educational, residential, and aquatic spaces. 
 
 Sustainable Materials for Demanding Environments 
 Manufactured from 100% polyester fiber—a blend of 60% post-consumer recycled PET and 40% virgin PET—Poly Max panels are: 
 
 Non-toxic, non-allergenic, and formaldehyde-free 
 Resistant to mold, mildew, and fungi 
 Certified for low VOC emissions (CDPH V1.2 compliant) 
 Contributing toward LEED v4.1 EQ Credit: Low-Emitting Materials 
 
   
 The core PET composition offers significant advantages over traditional acoustic materials: 
 
 Hydrophobic and non-hygroscopic, ensuring an extremely low moisture absorption rate 
 Maintains dimensional stability in high-humidity environments (e.g., natatoriums) 
 Will not swell, sag, or degrade under prolonged moisture exposure 
 
   
 Proven Durability in Aquatic and Humid Environments 
 Poly Max is specifically suited for high-humidity, chemically exposed environments like indoor pools and aquatic centers: 
 
 Microbial Resistance: PET is a non-organic, moisture-repellent substrate that resists mold and mildew growth. Verified through ASTM C1338 fungal resistance testing, Poly Max achieved the highest possible rating—zero fungal growth. 
 Chemical Resistance: PET is chemically stable and highly resistant to airborne chloramines and cleaning agents. Unlike metal or organic-based products, it will not corrode, oxidize, or break down, ensuring long-term performance and visual appeal . 
 
 
 Design Versatility 
 Poly Max panels balance affordability and architectural appeal with customization options that support diverse design intents: 
 
 Available in a wide range of colors, plus custom-printed graphics or photo-quality imagery 
 Tackable surface ideal for workspaces and classrooms 
 Engravable for added visual texture, branding, or artwork integration

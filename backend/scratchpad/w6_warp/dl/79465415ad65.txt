TECHNICAL DATA SHEET
Avery Dennison™ OF 2429 Is a single coated heat sensitive clear PE film coated with a blue
acrylic adhesive. It is used in the Optical industry and it is designed for the fastening and protection
of lenses during the manufacturing process.


FEATURES
●​ Specially designed pigmented adhesive
●​ Heat sensitive clear PE film carrier
●​ Medium release liner
●​ Removability


BENEFITS
●​ Adhere properly onto the lens and the alloy part
●​ Highly conformable to convex substrates
●​ Deblock easily from the alloy part
●​ Peel off cleanly from the lens leaving no residue
●​ Act as a shield between the warm alloy part and the (fragile) lens.

APPLICATION TECHNIQUES
     ●​ It is essential, as with all pressure-sensitive tapes, that the surface to which the tape is applied be
        clean, dry, and free of grease or oil
     ●​ Bond strength is dependent upon the amount of adhesive-to-surface contact developed
     ●​ Note that different pressure, time and temperature on different (film / rigid) surface achieves different
        performance

STORAGE / SHELF LIFE
When stored between 15°C and 30°C, relative humidity between 30% and 70%, out of direct sunlight, in
original packaging:

     ●​ Tape performance (adhesion, tack, release) can be guaranteed for 2 years after shipment.
     ●​ Absence of discoloration from the roll edge can be guaranteed for 1 year when stored in a dark place in
        the unopened original packaging

     DESCRIPTION

     LEXAN™ FR700 is a black, one side fine matt, one side velvet flame-retardant polycarbonate film with a UL94 V-0
     listing at 0.250 mm. This film has formability, excellent mechanical properties, good dimensional stability at high
     temperatures and a high flammability rating, making it good for applications such as power supply insulation, disc
     drive insulation, bus-bar insulation, TV/monitor insulation, PC board insulation, business equipment insulation and
     has insulation and EMI/RFI shielding when laminated with metal foil.

“PGS” Graphite Sheets
Type: EYG

PGS (Pyrolytic Graphite Sheet) is a thermal interface
material which is very thin, synthetically made, has
high thermal conductivity, and is made from a higly
oriented graphite polymer film. It is ideal for providing
thermal management/heat-sinking in limited spaces
or to provide supplemental heat-sinking in addition to
conventional means. This material is flexible and can
be cut into customizable shapes.

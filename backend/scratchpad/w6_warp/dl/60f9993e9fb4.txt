Product advantages
• Excellent initial adhesion (tack)
• High final adhesion
• Resistance to temperature, UV radiation, weathering, ageing, solvents and plasticizers
• DuploCOLL® 9042 has a vibration dampening effect.
• DuploCOLL® 9042 fulfills specifications in the automotive industry for interior and exterior applications.


Main application fields
• Fastening of nameplates and emblems in the automotive industry
• Mounting of slightly uneven substrates

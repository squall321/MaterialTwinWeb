     DUPONT™ KAPTON® MT+
     THERMALLY CONDUCTIVE POLYIMIDE FILM



DESCRIPTION                                                                                              APPLICATIONS
DuPont™ Kapton® MT+ polyimide film is a homogeneous film                                                 •       Thermal interface material substrate
possessing nearly 2x the thermal conductivity of Kapton® MT,                                             •       Heater circuits
while retaining superior electrical properties. Its thermal                                              •       Power supplies
conductivity properties make it ideal for use in controlling and                                         •       Ceramic board replacement
managing heat in electronic assemblies and as a substrate for
thermal interface materials.                                                                             FEATURES
                                                                                                         •       Highest thermal conductivity for a polyimide film
Kapton® MT+ offers an excellent combination of electrical                                                •       High mechanical integrity
properties, thermal conductivity, and mechanical toughness for its                                       •       Extended storage between 4-29°C (40-85°F)
use in electronic and automotive applications.                                                           •       UL 94 V-0 flammability rating
                                                                                                         •       RoHS Compliance

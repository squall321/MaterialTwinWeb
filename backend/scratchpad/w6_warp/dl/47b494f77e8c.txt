Product advantages
• Maximum resistance to ageing, UV-radiation, weathering and plasticizers.
• The foam carrier does not turn yellow and keeps a permanent elasticity.
• The high initial adhesion (tack) offers a reliable bond immediately after application. Maximum final adhesion is reached after
  approx. 24 hours.
• DuploCOLL® 918 absorbs vibration and compensates for different expansion coefficients of the substrates that are joined.
• DuploCOLL® 918 is recommended by manufacturers of glass and plastic mirrors on account of its inert behavior in contact with
  a wide variety of mirror paints.


Main application fields
• Mounting mirrors in the manufacture of bedroom, living room, bathroom and hall furniture
• Mounting of PVC stay bars
• Mounting of small devices and hooks on tiles and smooth walls
• Mounting of cable channels

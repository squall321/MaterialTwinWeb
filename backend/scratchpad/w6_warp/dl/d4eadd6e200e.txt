Autex Cube™ is a lightweight semi-rigid panel made with
solid colour throughout, offering design flexibility without the
need for edging or capping. Cube will help increase privacy
in open offices, reduce reverberated sound, and assist in
controlling background chatter noise.

Cube is made from polyester (which is recyclable) and
manufactured in New Zealand under Autex’s ISO 14001
certified Environmental Management System. Cube
contains a minimum of 65% post-consumer recycled
material (PET bottle-flake).

Fabric Care					
Blot spills from fabric quickly. Wipe with a damp cloth.
Avoid rubbing and excessive amounts of water as this will
affect the fabric finish. Use carpet or upholstery shampoo
as directed. Blot with a clean dry cloth after each
application of solution.

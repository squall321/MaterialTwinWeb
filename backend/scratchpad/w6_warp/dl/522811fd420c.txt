 Dimensional Stability
 The dimensional stability of Kapton® polyimide film depends on two factors–the normal coefficient of thermal
 expansion and the residual stresses placed in the film during manufacture. The latter causes Kapton® to shrink
 on its first exposure to elevated temperatures as indicated in the bar graph in Figure 1. Once the film has been
 exposed, the normal values of the thermal coefficient of linear expansion as shown in Table 4 can be expected.

Product advantages
• Light tack, permanent adhesive on both sides of a thin film carrier.
• Designed for use on “bubble bags” and very thin films where a permanent, but not a tamper evident closure is needed.
• The adhesive may be off-set to the left or right..
• Can be provided with a paper or polypropylene release liner and is available in pancake rolls or cross-wound spools.



Main application fields
• Permanent poly bag closure systems.

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
The CoFe alloy VACOFLUX® 50 offers high permeabilities and
magnetic saturation paired with an intermediate electrical
resistivity of re = 0.42 µΩm. It is mainly supplied as solid
material.
VACOFLUX 50 is used to work out complex shaped solid parts     TYPICAL APPLICATIONS
with the purpose of guiding particularly high and often also   Actuators with the highest forces/torques, yoke parts, pole
varying magnetic flux densities.                               shoes for very high flux densities, magnetic lenses, relay parts
